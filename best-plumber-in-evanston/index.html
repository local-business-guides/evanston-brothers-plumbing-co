From your first call to the plumbing work

The steps depend on the request. For a repair, describe the symptom and its location. For an installation, explain what you want installed and the area involved. A few useful things to do before service:
- Make the affected area accessible, if you can do so safely.
- Keep track of any new sounds, visible water, or changes you’ve noticed.
- Arrange for someone familiar with the issue to be available.

Don’t dismantle a fixture or try an unfamiliar repair just to prepare. If the situation feels urgent, call rather than waiting to collect every detail.
